# Table 1 — The anchor space (pre-registered knockout matrix)

Four norms behaviorally indistinguishable at rest, each defined by *which grounding channel, when removed, kills it*. The pattern is **diagonal**: every anchor dies to a different column and survives the others.

| Anchor | What holds it up | Enforcement removed (ghost) | Own grounding removed | Collapse shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Environmental ● | external payoff cost on the act | persists (+0.23) | decays (hazard off) | monotone, no tip |
| Coordination ● | payoff from matching others | **persists** (+0.20) | decays (returns flattened) | sharp, hysteretic (critical mass) |
| Pure vestige ● | nothing — enforcement only | **decays** (+0.01) | — (nothing to remove) | monotone, no critical point |
| Shibboleth ○ | in-group signalling benefit | persists if interaction live | decays (group structure removed) | bistable by group |

● established (computed; endpoint persistence, ghost-end gap at N=12, in parentheses).
○ predicted (stretch row; needs a pairwise-interaction gate the substrate does not yet implement).

The empty "own grounding" cell for pure vestige is not a gap — having nothing to remove but enforcement **is** the definition of a vestige.
